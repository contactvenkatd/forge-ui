Forge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Forge ("the app") is a design tool that generates 3D models and bills of materials from natural-language descriptions. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google, via Supabase Authentication.
  • Design data: the text descriptions you submit, the generated 3D model code, and bill-of-materials data are stored in your account so you can revisit past projects.

How information is used

  • To let you sign in and keep your project history across sessions.
  • To generate 3D models and materials lists by sending your design descriptions to an AI model provider (xAI/Grok) for processing.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for advertising.

Data storage

Account and project data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider. Data is retained until you delete your account, at which point your projects and account information are permanently removed.

Third-party services

  • Google — used for sign-in (OAuth). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — used for authentication and data storage. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • xAI (Grok) — used to process design descriptions and generate models. See xAI's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all associated projects at any time from the Settings panel within the app.
